# too-many-words-b

> Hook variant B of too-many-words: same body and voice, hook "The point is hiding in bullet *two*" (error). Post days apart from A.

## Angle
- Audience: operators, managers and juniors who send decks up to a boss; founders sending investor updates
- Pain or payoff (one sentence): the slide is five bullets and the point is buried in one of them, so the room reads the title and moves on
- Format: slide: "Q3 update" with five bullets → bullet two lights (the point was there all along) → on the turn the bullets clear, the title becomes the point and the chart proves it → the rule as ticks → recap
- The turn: [beat] [emphatic] You found it last. So say it first. (Minto: think bottom up, present top down)
- Why it could spread (who sends this to whom): the PowerPoint feedback loop is the niche's most shared pain (Joe Fenti's "Too many words!" skits, 4.1M views); managers send it to the person whose deck they just reviewed

## Hooks (pick one, keep the others for variants)
1. Your boss didn't read your *bullets* (problem, recognition) — chosen
2. The point is hiding in bullet *two* (error)
3. You found the point last. Say it *first*. (insider, Minto)
