# vibe-prompt

## Angle
- Source: @seeconvm's X article on Opus 5.5 "one-prompt" videos (x.com/seeconvm/status/2106096193235259564): the viral ones ran on ~9,500-character briefs; a vibe prompt leaves every blank to "the safest default it knows"
- Audience: anyone asking ChatGPT/Copilot/Claude for slides and getting generic ones
- Pain or payoff (one sentence): "Make me a deck on Q3" gets a default slide because the AI fills every blank you left; three lines it can't guess (the answer, who reads it, the real numbers) get a real slide
- Format: two prompts, two slides. Hook → vibe prompt typed → the generic topic slide it makes → turn → brief prompt (answer, audience, numbers) → Occam slide (shot) → recap
- The turn: [beat] [emphatic] It didn't fail. It guessed. Give it what only you know.
- Why it could spread: AI-curious crowd riding the "one prompt" wave; managers send it to whoever pastes AI slides
- Series: watch it fix it (AI slides)
- Business potential: 3: the brief prompt is typed into Occam's chat and the finished slide is Occam's

## Hooks (pick one, keep the others for variants)
1. The "one prompt" AI demos are *hiding* something (contrarian, rides the trend) — recommended
2. "Make me a deck" is why your AI slides look *generic* (problem)
3. Your AI slide isn't bad. It *guessed*. (error/recognition)
