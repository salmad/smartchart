# whats-the-so-what

## Angle
- Audience: juniors and operators getting slide reviews; anyone new to consulting-style feedback
- Pain or payoff (one sentence): "what's the so what?" sounds like a riddle; it means your title says what the chart is, not what it says
- Format: Boss feedback, decoded. Quote hook → topic title retitled to the claim → a three-card deck climbing from topic to claim to decision → recap
- The turn: [beat] [emphatic] So what? Tell me what it says.
- Why it could spread (who sends this to whom): consulting jargon every new joiner hears and nobody explains; managers send it to juniors

## Hooks (pick one, keep the others for variants)
1. "What's the *so what*?" (recognition, quote) — chosen
2. Your boss keeps asking "so *what*?" (problem)
3. Ask your chart *so what* twice (challenge)
