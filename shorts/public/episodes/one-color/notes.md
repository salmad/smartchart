# one-color

## Angle
- Audience: consultants, analysts, anyone who makes charts for leadership
- Pain or payoff (one sentence): charts where every bar has its own colour, so nothing stands out and the reader has to hunt for the point
- Format: slide: a rainbow bar chart → everything turns grey → one bar lights gold on the turn; then a quick before/after; recap
- The turn: [beat] [emphatic] Grey everything... except the one bar that matters.
- Why it could spread (who sends this to whom): instantly visible before/after; people send it to the colleague whose charts look like a bag of Skittles

## Hooks (pick one, keep the others for variants)
1. Grey is the most *important* colour on a slide (contrarian) — recommended
2. Your chart has too many *colours* (problem)
3. Make *one* bar do all the talking (result)
