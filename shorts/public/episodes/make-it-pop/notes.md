# make-it-pop

## Angle
- Audience: anyone whose boss reviews their slides; operators, juniors, analysts
- Pain or payoff (one sentence): "make it pop" sends people adding colour and effects, when it means the reader can't find the point
- Format: Boss feedback, decoded. Quote hook → rainbow chart (the usual response) → on the turn everything greys, one bar lights and the title becomes the point → the fix as ticks → recap
- The turn: [beat] [emphatic] Because pop means... I can't find the point.
- Why it could spread (who sends this to whom): the most quoted piece of slide feedback there is; people send it to the colleague who just got it, or to the boss who gave it

## Hooks (pick one, keep the others for variants)
1. "Can you make it *pop*?" (recognition, quote) — chosen
2. "Make it pop" means one *thing* (decoder)
3. More colour won't make it *pop* (contrarian)
