# due-in-ten

## Angle
- Audience: founders and operators with a deck due today; anyone who has built slides at 1am
- Pain or payoff (one sentence): you have the notes but no slide, and ten minutes; one prompt turns the notes into a slide that makes the point
- Format: Watch it fix it (Gamma's 'acceleration' spectacle: prompt to finished slide). Hook → wall-of-notes bullets → prompt typed into Occam → shot of the finished Occam slide → checks → recap
- The turn: [beat] [emphatic] One prompt. The answer goes in the title.
- Why it could spread (who sends this to whom): everyone has a deadline story; people send it to the colleague who always pulls all-nighters
- Series: watch it fix it
- Business potential: 3: the fix is done in Occam; shot of the real editor with the slide it made, and the prompt in Occam's chat

## Hooks (pick one, keep the others for variants)
1. Your deck is due in *ten* minutes (problem, POV) — recommended
2. I turned *these notes* into a consulting slide (result)
3. Stop building slides from *scratch* (contrarian)
