# walk-me-through-it

## Angle
- Audience: operators and founders who send decks ahead of a meeting
- Pain or payoff (one sentence): "walk me through it" means the deck couldn't stand on its own: the titles name sections instead of telling the story
- Format: Boss feedback, decoded. Quote hook → deck of topic titles (no story) → the turn as one line → deck of claim titles lighting in order → recap
- The turn: [beat] [emphatic] It means: your titles should tell it.
- Why it could spread (who sends this to whom): every pre-read deck gets this reply; people send it before their next pre-read

## Hooks (pick one, keep the others for variants)
1. "Walk me *through* it." (recognition, quote) — chosen
2. Your deck needs *you* to explain it (problem)
3. A deck that walks *itself* (result)
