# so-what-test

## Angle
- Audience: anyone who writes bullet slides; the sequel for viewers of too-many-words (its pinned comment)
- Pain or payoff (one sentence): bullets pile up because nothing filters them; one question decides which stay
- Format: slide with a claim title and five bullets → the two that prove the title light, the rest dim → recap
- The turn: [beat] [emphatic] Ask each one: so what? Does it prove the title?
- Why it could spread (who sends this to whom): a reusable test people save and apply to their next slide; follows too-many-words

## Hooks (pick one, keep the others for variants)
1. Ask every bullet *one* question (did-you-know) — chosen
2. Three of these bullets *don't belong* (error)
3. The test consultants run on every *bullet* (insider)
