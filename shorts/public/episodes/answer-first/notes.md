# answer-first

## Angle
- Audience: consultants, managers presenting to executives
- Pain or payoff (one sentence): decks that build up to the answer on slide 12; executives stop listening by slide 3
- Format: deck: titles in 'story order' (background, analysis, …, answer last) → on the turn the answer slide flies to the top and the rest re-order as support; recap
- The turn: [beat] [emphatic] Partners flip it. The answer goes first.
- Why it could spread (who sends this to whom): every junior has presented a 'build-up' deck; the reorder animation makes the idea click in one second

## Hooks (pick one, keep the others for variants)
1. Your deck is *upside down* (contrarian) — recommended
2. Stop saving the answer for slide *12* (problem)
3. Consultants start with the *ending* (insider)
