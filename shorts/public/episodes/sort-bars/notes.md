# sort-bars

## Angle
- Audience: anyone who makes bar charts in Excel or PowerPoint
- Pain or payoff (one sentence): bars left in alphabetical (or random) order, so the reader can't see the ranking or the outlier
- Format: slide: bars in A–Z order → on the turn they re-sort largest to smallest and the leader lights gold; recap
- The turn: [beat] [emphatic] Sort it. Biggest on top.
- Why it could spread (who sends this to whom): a one-click fix people can apply today; the re-sort animation is satisfying to watch twice

## Hooks (pick one, keep the others for variants)
1. Alphabetical order is *hiding* your story (problem) — recommended
2. One click makes this chart *readable* (result)
3. Why consultants never sort A to *Z* (insider)
