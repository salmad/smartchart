**Notes on reading it**
- v1 recorded its turn time after the judgment checks, the prototype at the reply (fixed after this run: `turn.ts` now stamps the time at the reply). So v1's latency above is overstated by roughly the judgment time, and v1 is still faster.
- Time to the reply from the step traces (both apps measured the same way): new slide p50 20.4 s (prototype) vs 17.1 s (v1); edit 4.5 s vs 4.2 s. GLM ran faster during the v1 run (32 vs 28 tokens/s).
- Jev calls looked ~250 ms slower in the v1 run; the same Jev request sent 10 times alternately through both servers took 0.31 s (prototype server) vs 0.29 s (v1 dev relay), so that gap was the API at a different time, not the app. Measuring a slide in the v1 page takes 1.3 ms.
- Edit e02 was rewritten for the Acme data (interchange is 85 in year 5 after the chart-notes fix); e04's miss is the test checking series position 4 while the agent put the new bars before the margin line.
- Jev routed 4 of 6 chart requests to the labelled mark in the final run, 6 of 6 in the two earlier runs (the 788f4d7 baseline report had 5 of 6): router variance on c11/c12 (bar vs line).
- Red/green: none in titles or subtitles; red/green values or cards 8 → 5 after the guidance change.

**Verdict:** no regression. Quality is equal or better on every measure except Jev routing variance; time to reply is lower; the app adds no measurable time.
